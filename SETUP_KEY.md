## Brand positioning and voice

### One-sentence brand summary

Vibe Smoke & Supply Co is a veteran-owned neighborhood shop bringing a clean, comfortable, and friendly smoke, vape, accessory, and everyday-essentials experience to adults 21+ in Punta Gorda.

### Brand promise

- A considered mix of smoke, vape, accessories, and everyday essentials.
- Friendly help without pressure.
- A clean, comfortable store built for local customers.
- Responsible service for adults 21 and older.

### Voice

- Local, upbeat, welcoming, direct, and confident.
- Short sentences and plain language.
- Coastal energy without sounding touristy or gimmicky.
- Always mature and responsible; never youth-oriented.

### Approved phrases

- Punta Gorda's New Vibe.
- Built for locals. Stocked right.
- Veteran owned. Punta Gorda proud.
- Friendly help. No pressure.
- Your essentials, all in one stop.
- Adults 21+ only. Please enjoy responsibly.

### Avoid

- Health, wellness, or safety claims about vape or smoke products.
- Youth-oriented language, imagery, trends, or characters.
- Claims that inventory, prices, or brands are available unless confirmed.
- Cannabis, dispensary, medical, or therapeutic language unless the business later confirms it is legally applicable.
- Different spellings of the company name or different address-unit formats.
